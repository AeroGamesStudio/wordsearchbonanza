Frequently asked questions

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On your old phone, open Settings (the sliders button), tap Back up in the Progress section, then copy the code or share it to yourself (Notes or email work well). On your new phone, open Settings > Restore, paste the code, and confirm. Your levels, coins, companions, outfits, and achievements come back. Your "Remove ads" purchase is separate: use Restore purchase (below).

How do I get my "Remove ads" purchase back?

Open Settings, find the Ads section, and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you used to buy it. The purchase is tied to that account, not to a backup code.

Why do I see ads?

Word Search Bonanza is free to play. A short ad appears after every third completed level in regular levels. Timed Challenge and the Daily Puzzle never show ads. You can remove ads for good with a one-time purchase in Settings or the Shop.

My progress or coins disappeared. What can I do?

First try closing and reopening the game. If you made a backup code earlier, you can restore it from Settings. Progress is stored on your device, so deleting the app removes it unless you backed it up. If you still need help, contact us with the details below.

How does the daily reward work?

Claim a reward once per day. Claim on consecutive days to move along the 7-day calendar to bigger rewards. If you skip a full day, the calendar starts again at Day 1.

How does the Daily Puzzle work?

Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. Finishing it earns coins, and finishing on consecutive days builds a streak that increases the reward.

What do companions and bond levels do?

Each companion has its own personality and a perk, such as free hints or extra time in Timed Challenge. Playing with a companion equipped raises your bond with it, which makes its perk stronger. You can change companions and outfits in the Shop.

What accessibility options are there?

In Settings you can choose a highlight color (yellow, orange, blue, or pink), turn on High contrast, turn on Reduce motion, and switch to Tap two letters to select instead of swiping. You can also change the text size. The letter grid has labels for screen readers (VoiceOver and TalkBack), and hints say exactly where a word starts.

I can't hear any sound.

Check the Music and Sound effects switches and sliders in Settings. Also check your phone's media volume. On iPhone, make sure the silent switch is not on, because iPhone can silence game audio when the ringer is set to silent.

How do I start over?

Open Settings > Reset and confirm twice. This erases your progress on this device but keeps your sound and text settings and your Remove ads purchase. Make a backup first if you might want your progress back.
