Effective date: October 12, 2026

Summary

Word Search Bonanza keeps your game progress on your device and does not ask you to create an account. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage information (see Advertising below). You can turn ads off permanently with a one-time "Remove ads" purchase.

Who we are

Word Search Bonanza is published by Trey Miller under the name Aero Mobile Studios. You can reach us at aeromobilestudios@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts, and the game has no sign-in or social features. We do not receive your game progress or any personal data. The only information collected about your use of the game comes from the advertising service described under Advertising below, and only while ads are turned on.

Information stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • Your current level and puzzle progress
  • Your coin balance, companions, outfits, and bond levels
  • Your achievements, daily reward streak, and Daily Puzzle progress
  • Your settings, such as sound, text size, and accessibility choices
  • Whether you have removed ads
  • Your Daily Puzzle reminder setting and time, if you turn reminders on

This game data stays on your device. We do not send it to any server of ours, and deleting the app deletes it. Your "Remove ads" purchase is recorded by Apple (under your Apple ID) or Google Play (under your Google account), not by us, and can be restored.

Backup codes. The Back up tool in Settings creates a code on your device that contains your saved progress. We do not receive or store these codes. Anyone who has your code could restore your progress, so keep it private.

Daily reminders. If you turn on the Daily Puzzle reminder, your device schedules the reminders itself. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Sharing and ratings. "Share result" only shares the text you choose to send, using your phone's own share menu. When the game invites you to rate it, the rating window is shown by Apple or Google, and your rating goes to them, not to us.

Third parties

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads, and Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) process purchases. These services handle your information under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your information.

Advertising

The free version shows a full-screen ad after every few completed levels. When an ad is shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), IP address, device type and operating system, and which ads you view or tap. They use it to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This information goes to them, not to us.

On iPhone, the game asks whether you allow tracking. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. In some places, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and US states with their own privacy laws, Google's privacy message lets you choose how your information is used for ads. Where this applies, an "Ad privacy choices" button appears in the game's Settings so you can review or change your choice at any time, including opting out of targeted advertising (which some laws call the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information). You can change your tracking choice any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalization in your device's Settings (look under Privacy or Google settings, then Ads). Buying "Remove ads" stops ads completely.

See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

The game offers one optional one-time purchase, "Remove ads", which permanently turns off ads. Apple or Google (depending on your device) handles the payment, and we never see or store your payment details. Coins in the game are earned by playing and cannot be bought. To get the purchase back on another device signed in with the same Apple ID, use "Restore purchase" in Settings. On iPhone this uses the same Apple ID, and on Android it uses the same Google account.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no chat, no user-generated content, and no links to outside websites other than through ads. If you believe a child's information has reached us or an ad provider, contact us at the email below.

Your choices

You can erase your saved game data by deleting the app or using Reset in Settings. You can decline tracking when iOS asks and change that later in iOS Settings, or manage ad personalization in your Android device's settings. Where it applies, you can also use "Ad privacy choices" in the game's Settings. You can turn ads off for good with the "Remove ads" purchase.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you rights over personal information, such as the right to ask what is held about you or to have it deleted. We do not hold personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you can opt out of targeted advertising by tapping "Ad privacy choices" in the game's Settings, or by turning off ad tracking or personalization in your device settings. For information held by advertising partners, you can also use Google's own privacy tools. You can also contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect.
